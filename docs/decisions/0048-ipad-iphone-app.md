# 0048. An iPad and iPhone app

Date: 2026-11-25
Status: Accepted. Its rule that new features land on every platform is superseded by [0051](0051-mobile-workflow.md).

## Context
Issue #218. The roadmap named an iPad and iPhone app under Later. `ProofKit` was kept free of AppKit, UIKit and SwiftUI so that app could reuse it, `Package.swift` already lists iOS 27, and `FileAccess` takes a `BookmarkSystem` for folders picked in the Files app. The app should open one or many folders from Files, offer every adjustment the Mac app has, and follow Apple's guidelines for iPadOS and iOS in navigation, layout, controls and gestures.

What isn't ready: `FolderWatcher` uses FSEvents, which iOS doesn't have, so `ProofKit` doesn't build for iOS. The Mac app's model, `Library`, lives in the Mac target and uses AppKit in a few places (thumbnail images, mount notifications, Finder, the pasteboard). The canvas and the tools drawn on the photo (crop, masks, spot removal, the curve) are built on `NSView` and `NSEvent`.

## Decision
- One universal app for iPad and iPhone, shipped in one release. It keeps Proof's rules: a dark interface, the canvas color, #FFD52E only for small marks of state, panels beside the photo and never over it, Browse and Edit as separate modes, and folders shown as they are on disk.
- **Navigation.** One `NavigationSplitView`. In regular width (iPad) its sidebar lists All Photos, the filter rows and the saved folders as trees with counts, as on the Mac, beside the grid. In compact width (iPhone, and iPad windows made narrow) it collapses into a stack: library, then grid, then photo, with the standard back button and edge swipe. The app has one window, as on the Mac, and lays out again as the window is resized.
- **Folders from Files.** Add Folder, in the sidebar's toolbar and in the empty state's `ContentUnavailableView`, opens the system folder picker (`fileImporter` for folders, several at once). Any location Files shows works: on the device, iCloud Drive, USB and SD drives, SMB servers and file-provider apps. Bookmarks are kept by `FileAccess`. A folder on an unplugged drive is offline; one that moved or was deleted is missing and has Locate…. A photo in iCloud that isn't downloaded shows a cloud badge and downloads when opened. Folders are read again when the app returns to the foreground and on pull to refresh, since iOS has no FSEvents.
- **Browse.** A `LazyVGrid` of thumbnails with the same badges; pinching changes the thumbnail size. Tap opens Edit. Touch and hold opens the context menu: rating, flag, Copy and Paste Settings, Export, Info. Select in the toolbar starts multiple selection, and dragging across thumbnails selects a range, as in Photos. The filter is a toolbar menu, shown only in Browse.
- **Edit on iPad.** The photo sits centered on the canvas and the adjustments are in a trailing `.inspector` with the Mac's cards in the Mac's order, built from `Slider`, `Toggle` and `Picker`. Double-tapping a slider resets it. The toolbar has Done, rating and flag menus, Undo and Redo, Crop, Spot Removal, Masks, History, Share and the inspector button. The filmstrip runs under the photo and can be hidden.
- **Edit on iPhone.** The same cards, in the same order, scroll in a sheet below the photo. This is how `.inspector` presents in compact width, so both devices share one inspector. The sheet has a small and a medium height, and the photo resizes to fit above it, so the sheet never covers it. The photo stays usable while the sheet is up (`presentationBackgroundInteraction`). Crop, Spot Removal and Masks show their controls in the sheet in place of the cards, as they do in the Mac inspector. In landscape on a phone with regular width the inspector is a trailing column, as on iPad. History and Info open as sheets.
- **Gestures.** Pinch zooms around the fingers, double-tap switches between Fit and 100%, a drag pans when zoomed, touching and holding the photo shows the original, and a horizontal swipe moves to the next or previous photo when the photo is at Fit and no tool is open. Apple Pencil paints masks and spot strokes, without pressure by default, and Pencil hover shows the brush's outline. On iPad with a keyboard, the Mac's shortcuts work and its commands are in the menu bar.
- **Export.** Share opens the export sheet with the Mac's choices (format, size, color space, Remove Location), then the share sheet, Save to Files, or Save to Photos. Saving to Photos asks for add-only access the first time. Long batches keep running in the background with their progress shown.
- **Looks.** `.cube` files and folders are imported from Files into the app's LUT library and previewed as on the Mac.
- **Accessibility.** Every control and badge has a VoiceOver label, lists and the inspector follow Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion is respected, and touch targets are at least 44 points.
- **Code.** `ProofKit` stays as it is, apart from a folder-change source for iOS in place of FSEvents. A new package target, `ProofUI`, holds the SwiftUI code both apps share: `Library`, with its AppKit calls moved behind small platform hooks and its images as `CGImage`, and the views that are already plain SwiftUI. Moving `Library` doesn't change how the Mac app behaves. A Proof iOS target in `App/Proof.xcodeproj` adds the touch views: the Metal canvas in a `UIViewRepresentable`, and touch versions of the crop, mask, spot and curve tools. CI builds `ProofKit` for iOS, runs its tests on an iPhone simulator, and builds the iOS app.
- The work is the iPad and iPhone milestone, one issue per step.
- From here on, new features land on every platform. A feature fitting one small PR ships on all of them in it. A larger one is split by layer: the engine, shared parts and the first platform in one issue, the other platform's input and layout in a paired issue filed at the same time in the same milestone. While the iOS app doesn't yet have the area a feature belongs to, the feature is added to the iOS issue for that area. CLAUDE.md states the rule.

## Consequences
- The iPad layout matches the Mac's, and the iPhone reuses the same inspector, so a new adjustment is added once for every device.
- Folders on network shares and USB drives aren't watched on iOS; changes show when the app returns to the foreground or the grid is refreshed.
- A 60 MP RAW decode is large for an iPhone's memory. Phones may need a lower decode scale while editing; export still renders full size. This is measured on a device before release.
- Moving `Library` into `ProofUI` touches the Mac app and must be a move without behavior changes.
- Installing on devices and TestFlight need signing for iOS, which is decided in its own issue.
